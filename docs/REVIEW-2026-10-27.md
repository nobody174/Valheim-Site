## HIGH

### H1. "Hoard or trash" advice contradicts the recipes
17 items are marked `safe_discard` or `decorative` but are ingredients:
- TrophyDeer is `decorative`, yet it summons Eikthyr (2) and is in Stagbreaker (5 + upgrades). The live Deer Trophy page shows both "Decorative" and "Used to summon Eikthyr".
- Honey, Raspberry, Blueberries (10 each in mead bases), RoundLog/Corewood (Finewood bow, Stagbreaker, bronze pickaxe, Cultivator), Thistle (Sausages), Carrot, Mushroom, MushroomYellow, Dandelion, RawMeat, DeerMeat, NeckTail, CookedDeerMeat and the three Mead Bases are all `safe_discard`.
- All 80 crafted items default to `safe_discard`, including Hammer, Hoe, Cultivator, Torch, Fishing Rod.
Fix: derive the default from the graph and add a validate rule: an item used as a recipe, upgrade, station or summon input can never be `safe_discard` or `decorative`. Tools you must own should be `keep`.

### H2. The builder silently drops recipes (root cause of fire arrows)
`tools/extract/build_data.py`: a recipe whose output is not allowlisted is skipped with no message (around line 272). A recipe with a non-allowlisted input only warns (around line 281). Fire arrows and early axes were lost this way.
Fix: make the build print and CI-diff two lists: (a) game recipes whose inputs are all allowlisted but whose output is not, (b) allowlisted outputs whose recipe was dropped. Add a test: every allowlisted craftable has a recipe or is in a documented `noRecipe` list.

### H3. Station levels cannot be used
- Every station has `levels: []`.
- Cauldron has no extensions, yet Sausages need a level 2 cauldron (wiki agrees). Nothing tells you how to reach it.
- Upgrades do not show a required station level per quality. The wiki shows workbench level rising with quality (Club: L1 up to L4). Likely rule is minLevel + quality - 1; verify in game.
