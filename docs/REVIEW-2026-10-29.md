## Check 4: data honesty

Things shown as fact that are not simply read from the game files, or are labelled inconsistently:

- **"Seen in game" is stamped on whole entries, but each confirmation covers one line.** A `gear:` key
  checks damage, armor and durability from the tooltip, yet marks the item, weapon and armor entries
  in-game. A `creature:` key checks drops only; `boss:` checks the summon and drops; `where:` checks only
  the hand-written "where to find" line. Weight, stack size, resistances and attacks are not part of any check (H14).
- **The confirmation is not tied to a value.** The file stores key and date, not the text that was
  confirmed. If a game patch or a later edit changes the expected line, the entry stays in-game (H14).
- **Boss summon lines are always tagged "guide, not checked"** (`ProgressionPage.tsx` line 99), even
  though they come from the game files and Eikthyr and The Elder were seen in game (H16).
- **Hoard badges** ("Must keep", "Keep some", "Safe to discard") are hand-curated advice per zone
  config and carry no guide tag. DATA_SOURCES says so; the badge itself does not.
- **Places** (79 entries) carry no `verified` or `source` field and are not on the Data trust page.
- Good: the per-value "guide, not checked" tag is still used on hand-written source lines, boss notes
  and progression notes. Batch 2 shows the hand-written lines do contain errors: two notes were corrected
  after the owner checked in game (a wrong "found in chests" and a wrong "no known source").

## New findings

Severity: High / Medium / Low. IDs continue from review 4.

- **H13 (Medium): the backup does not contain the Check in game answers.** `buildExport` writes
  profiles only. Check in game answers moved to a per-browser store, but the backup reminder counts
  them as "something to lose" and says your ticks live only in this browser, and clearing the browser
  loses them. Also, the reminder appears only when you have a character, progress or Check in game
  answers; portals, notes, favorites and hoard overrides do not trigger it. "Later" lasts until the next page reload.
- **H14 (Medium): "seen in game" is entry-wide, and not tied to a value.** See check 4. Suggest either
  per-aspect flags (for example `seenInGame: ["drops"]`) or relabelling the Trust tile to "at least one
  line seen in game", and storing the confirmed text (or a hash) next to the key so a patch invalidates it.
- **H15 (Medium): two different "seen in game" counts per zone.** The Meadows banner says 21 (items,
  creatures, bosses); the Trust table says 32 (also weapons, armor, foods, trophies). The zone table
  sums to 776 entries and 67 in game while the headline says 1,290 and 83: recipes, stations and
  pieces have no zone, and the 16 in-game recipes appear only in the headline. Say so on the page, or
  use one definition.
- **H16 (Low): boss summon lines are always "guide, not checked."** They come from the files, and two
  bosses were seen in game. Tie the tag to the boss's `verified` level.
- **H17 (Medium): portal rules.** See check 5: rule 3 and its "all off" status are unconfirmed and
  likely overstated; rule 4's world-setting sentence is untagged and misnamed; the status lines carry
  no tag; `portalBlocked` has no DATA_SOURCES entry.
- **H18 (Low): remaining touch gaps.** Gear link 20x29 on every page, Home chips 41px, Progression tabs
  42px, running-text links 20px, the portals checkbox label 30px, item-page buttons 38px.
- **H19 (Low): the 390px axe `region` item and `/skills` `landmark-unique`.** Both are one-line fixes.
- **H20 (Low): ROADMAP numbers are typed by hand.** "73 entries" is already wrong. Print the counts
  from `patchcheck` or the Trust page instead.
- **H21 (Low): QA is still manual.** The audit script is not in `tools/qa/` or CI, and today's run
  needed an emulation to stand in for a touch device. A Playwright script in CI would keep the zeros.

## The single most valuable thing to do next

**Verify the hand-written "where to find it" lines while you play, one zone at a time, starting with
the zone you are in now, and make confirming one a single tap on the item page.**

Why this and not something else:

- 53 of the 60 unverified entries are exactly these lines. Everything else on the site is read from
  the game files. They are also what you look up on a second monitor ("where do I get X?").
- They are the lines most likely to be wrong, and batch 2 proved it: of the notes checked, two were
  corrected on the spot.
- The Check in game page works but asks you to leave the page you are using. A "matches / doesn't
  match" button next to the line on the item page turns normal lookups into verification, with no
  extra session. It needs H13 first (a backup that includes the answers) and H14 (confirm the value, not the entry),
  both small.

Everything else on this list (tap targets, virtualisation, a landing sentence) is polish next to that.
