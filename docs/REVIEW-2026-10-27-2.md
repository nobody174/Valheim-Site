- Mobile was tested with emulation, not on a real phone. Contrast was tested by axe only, not by eye on a phone in sunlight.

## 1. Findings, ordered by severity

### HIGH

**F1. Smelter, Charcoal Kiln, Blast Furnace and Oven have no build cost, and the page gives a reason that is no longer true** (area: data)
- Where: `/#/station/smelter`, `data/stations.json`, `tools/extract/biomes/swamp.json`.
- What: the page says "Unknown for now (it needs items from biomes not added yet)". The Swamp is added. The word "Surtling" appears nowhere in `/data` or the biome configs: no Surtling creature, no Surtling Core item. A public guide (eip.gg, not verified in game) gives 20 Stone + 5 Surtling Cores for the Smelter and Kiln, plus 10 Iron, 20 Fine Wood and an Artisan Table for the Blast Furnace.
- Why it matters: "what do I need to craft Bronze" ends at "Forge level 1" and then "Smelter: unknown". Every metal in the game goes through these stations. This is the single most-needed answer in the Black Forest and the site cannot give it.
- Fix: add Surtling (Swamp, with its drop) and Surtling Core to `swamp.json`, rebuild, and mark the where-to-get note unverified. Make the builder print a warning for any station with an empty `buildRequirements`. Update the stale wording in the station page.

**F2. The JavaScript bundle doubled to 2.07 MB (1.1 MB gzip) because 397 icons are embedded inside it** (area: code)
- Where: `src/data/icons.ts`, `vite.config.ts`. `dist/assets/index-*.js` contains 397 `data:image/webp;base64` strings; the PWA precache is 2.4 MB.
- What: Vite inlines files under 4 KB, and `import.meta.glob(..., { eager: true })` pulls all 444 icon files into the entry chunk. 158 of those 444 icons belong to entries that are not in the database yet (ships, carts, ingot stacks, Mistlands items) and are still bundled.
- Why it matters: the first visit on a phone over mobile data downloads and parses 1.1 MB before anything shows. Review 1 measured 929 kB, so this regressed.
- Fix (small): set `build.assetsInlineLimit: 0` so icons stay separate cached files; load icons lazily (`eager: false`) or filter the glob to ids in the data; delete or move the 158 unused icons (this also shrinks the copyrighted-art footprint, see F19). Re-measure; target under 700 kB gzip.

**F3. A returning player can run an old version for days without knowing** (area: UX, data trust)
- Where: `vite.config.ts` (`registerType: 'prompt'`), `src/app/UpdatePrompt.tsx`.
- What: my own Chrome served an older build in which the Rankings zone filter stopped at Swamp. A small corner toast said "A new version is available. Reload"; only after I clicked it did Mountains and Plains appear. On a phone the "Data: Steam build / Reviewed" line is not visible at all (it only exists in the desktop sidebar).
- Why it matters: for a site whose promise is "data trust", showing last week's recipes without saying so is the worst failure mode. A second-monitor player never sees a corner toast.
- Fix: use `registerType: 'autoUpdate'` (it is a static personal site, there is no unsaved work to lose beyond localStorage, which survives), or keep the prompt but make it a persistent banner. Show data version and "reviewed" date in the header or footer on every screen size.

**F4. Fenris Hair, Fenris Claw and Ymir Flesh are still unobtainable on the site, and a creature is probably missing** (area: data)
- Where: `data/items.json` (WolfHairBundle, WolfClaw, YmirRemains), `tools/extract/biomes/mountain.json`, `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` (Mountains pass).
- What: the site honestly says "Source not confirmed: no creature drops it in the game files". The Fenring entry drops only Wolf Fang and its trophy, which matches one public guide (gamewith). Yet the Fenris armor, Flesh Rippers and the Iron Sledge need these items, and the Fenris armor set is declared as 3 pieces but only 2 exist in the data (the helmet needs a "Cultist" trophy that is not in the database). The pattern (a crafting item with no source, a set missing a piece, a recipe waiting on a trophy that does not exist) is the same pattern as last time's missing bear. **My guess, not verified:** the Mountain cave creatures (a Fenring Cultist type) and possibly a Swamp crypt source for Ymir Flesh are not in the biome configs.
- Why it matters: three craftable endgame-of-zone items show "where found: unknown", and the set bonus line for Fenris will read as a 3-piece set that cannot be completed.
- Fix: grep the raw spawn tables and character drop tables for who drops `WolfHairBundle`, `WolfClaw` and `YmirRemains` before adding anything by hand; add the missing creatures; until then keep the "not confirmed" note and add a visible "no known source" badge on the item card.

**F5. Search gives nothing for short misspelled words and knows no alternative names** (area: UX)
- Where: `src/features/search/search.ts` lines 61-64.
- What: fuzzy matching is only on for words longer than 4 characters, and every word must match (`combineWith: 'AND'`). I typed "bronz swrd" and "fire arow" in the palette: zero results and no hint. "swrd" and "arow" are 4 letters, so fuzzy is off for them. `SearchDoc.aliases` exists but no item has any alias, so "Goblin" does not find Fuling, and old guide names do not find new names.
- Why it matters: search is the main interaction ("lookup speed"), and mid-fight typing on a phone produces typos.
- Fix: allow fuzzy for terms of 3+ characters with a small distance, fall back to OR matching when AND finds nothing, and show "No results. Did you mean ...?". Add `aliases` in the biome configs (Goblin/Fuling, Corewood/Core wood/Round log, Fenris/Wolf, Deer Hide/Deerskin, Bukeperries/Pukeberries, Black Metal/Blackmetal).

### MEDIUM

**F6. The item page buries the answer** (area: UX)
- Where: `src/features/items/ItemPage.tsx`; e.g. `/#/item/SwordIron`, `/#/item/Bronze`.
- What: the first block is "Your progress" (four chips, a dropdown, an explanatory sentence), then a table of weight, stack size, tags ("none") and the internal id ("SwordIron"), and only then the actual answers: "Crafted from" and "Where found". On a 390px phone the recipe is about three screens down.
- Fix: put "Where to get it" and "Crafted from" first; shrink progress and hoard to one compact toolbar row; hide the internal id and "Tags: none" behind a "details" fold.

**F7. Hoard advice default is meaningless as a default** (area: data, UX)
- Where: `tools/extract/build_data.py` hoard derivation; `data/items.json`.
- What: 143 of 272 items default to "Safe to discard", including 100 of 106 crafted weapons, armor and tools. `/#/item/SwordIron` shows a "Safe to discard" badge on the Iron Sword. Coins are "safe to discard" although Haldor sells things for coins (guides put the Fishing Rod around 300 coins, unverified).
- Why it matters: a badge that is true for half the database stops carrying information, and "safe to discard" next to the sword you are holding teaches the player to ignore the badge.
- Fix: crafted gear gets no badge (or "Gear") by default; the discard badge is reserved for things you could really throw away; Coins become "keep some".

**F8. Internal prefab names leak into player-facing text** (area: data)
- Where: `hoardNote` on 24 items, e.g. Fenris Hair: "Used for crafting ArmorFenringChest, crafting ArmorFenringLegs, crafting FistFenrirClaw and 3 more." Also the type filter and table on `/#/rankings` show "skill0" for the Ooze Bomb (`BombOoze` has skill id 0 which the builder maps to `skill0`).
- Fix: build the note from display names; map skill 0 to "None / throwable" or leave the Ooze Bomb out of the weapon ranking.

**F9. Recipes the builder knows about but left out, some now addable** (area: data)
- Where: `tools/extract/dropped_recipes.json` (30 entries).
- What: `Recipe_WolfSkewer` has all inputs in the database now (Wolf Meat, Mushroom, Onion) but is still dropped; the Plains pass note said it waited for Onion. The 7 trinkets (Bronze, Iron, Silver, Black Metal) are craftable at the Forge from items that exist, but are skipped because "a trinket category is not modelled". Fishing bait variants (Troll, Abomination, Fuling...) use trophies you can have. Black Forge items (bolts, Arbalest, Skoll and Hati knives) are missing, so the Black Forge is not a station at all.
- Why it matters: a player at the Forge will see trinkets in game and find nothing on the site; this is the same class of gap as last review's Fire Arrow.
- Fix: model trinkets (one category), re-run the dropped-recipes report in each zone PR and require every entry to be either added or given a reason in a new `dropped_recipes_reasons` list.

**F10. Rankings: default order, noise, and a full screen of warning on a phone** (area: UX, data)
- Where: `src/features/rankings/WeaponRankings.tsx`; `/#/rankings`.
- What: the list is ordered by damage per hit, so the Crystal Battleaxe is first with DPS 147 while the Silver Sword has DPS 213 and rating 94. The orange "DPS is an estimate" box is 8 lines on a phone, then six filters; the table starts two screens down. The list mixes in a shield bash (Banded Shield, "Per hit 10"), the Ooze Bomb, and the Stone Pickaxe (no known source). The Rating column is hidden on mobile. The three Swamp-and-later shields have `maxQuality > 1` but no per-level block power in the data, so the "Upgrade level" filter cannot change a shield's block (I did not verify what the page shows).
- Fix: default sort by rating or DPS; move the estimate note to a one-line badge with an info toggle; keep shields and consumables out of the weapon list (a separate Shield view); show Rating on mobile as a compact column.

**F11. "Unverified" is page-wide, not per fact, and the summon costs look like facts** (area: data, UX)

## 2. Part 1 summary: data trust

What was checked and how:
- **Hand-written claims**: all 56 `unverified` entries are the where-to-get notes, plus 5 bosses (summon items). They are marked, except at the places named in F11.
- **Weak spots you named**: Swamp/Mountain/Plains where-to-get notes are all `unverified` (good). Boss summon costs agree with one guide. Fenris Hair/Claw and Ymir Flesh have no source (F4). DPS is labelled an estimate on the page and in DATA_SOURCES (good) and the formulas on the page match the code in `attackModel.ts`, `weaponStats.ts` and `weaponRanking.ts` (damage per quality, resistance multipliers, skill range, combo multiplier, rating percentiles). Computed values are not stored in JSON.
- **Consistency scripts (all over `/data`)**: no dangling references; no creature drops an item from a later zone; every craftable has a recipe or a documented reason; armor sets match their declared size except Fenris (3 declared, 2 present); no duplicate item names; 15 materials are never used in any recipe (mostly seeds, Ruby, Amber, Wishbone, Yagluth Drop: expected for Mistlands or trinkets, but Wishbone and Yagluth Drop appear with no explanation).

Everything else is "extracted from game files, internally consistent, not verified by me". The cleanest way to close this is the in-game spot-check list already in DATA_SOURCES, extended to the three new zones; I suggest the owner ticks those off as zones are reached.
- **Missing creatures, foods, weapons a player would expect**: Surtling (Swamp), Fenring Cultist-type creature (Mountains, my guess), Draugr Archer (Swamp, from memory), Bat (Mountains caves, from memory), the trinkets (F9), Black Forge items, fishing bait variants. The last two lists are partly deliberate (documented), the first three are not on any documented list.

## 3. Part 2 summary: UX walk-through

Three-second test: Home (search box, clear), Items, Recipes (clear), Progression (clear), Rankings (clear but dense), Checklist (clear). Item pages fail the test (F6). Clicks for "what do I need to craft X": search, open item, scroll past progress and details to "Crafted from": 2 clicks and 2-3 screens of scrolling. For "where do I get Y": same, but it is a one-line note for hand-written sources.

Search: instant locally; palette closes with Escape and the X; no results for short typos (F5); results grouped by Items, Trophies, Creatures, Bosses, Stations. Plural and prefix work ("raspberr"). No alias support.

Tables and filters: URL holds the filters (shareable, good). Sorting is by column click. Mobile tables scroll inside their container, but the Building and Food tables cut text (F13).

Consistency: "Bukeperries" is the game's own spelling; "Mountains" (zone label) vs `mountain` (id) is fine. "Fuling" appears twice (F15). "Hoard" is the word used for "keep or discard" in the code and docs but pages say "Keep, stock up or throw away?" and badges say "Must keep", "Keep some", "Safe to discard", "Decorative", "Sell": pick one vocabulary.

Second-monitor annoyances: no "current zone" setting, so every list shows all five zones; no pinned or compact mode; the progress panel on every item page takes the top of the screen.

## 4. Part 3: design, polish and vibe

### Scores
- **Professional look: 6.5 / 10.** Consistent dark theme, restrained palette, readable Inter body with a Cinzel display face, tidy filters, good focus states and no layout bugs on desktop. It loses points for flat grey panels with identical 1 px borders on every page, near-empty home page, and a toast and tab strip that crowd the phone screen.
- **Feels like Valheim: 3 / 10.** The only Valheim in it is the item icons (which are great) and the Cinzel headings. Everything else (grey cards, amber links, dropdown forms) is a generic dark admin dashboard. No zone has any identity, nothing is wood, iron, parchment, ember or frost.

### Direction: "Longhouse at night"
Dark charcoal base (keep it, readable on a second monitor), with weathered wood, forged iron and parchment accents, and each zone lights the UI in its own colour.

Proposed tokens (extend `src/styles/tokens.css`; all combinations below target at least 4.5:1 text contrast on their background; run the same axe scan after applying):

Typography (all free, self-hosted through `@fontsource` so offline keeps working): keep **Cinzel** for page titles; add **Uncial Antiqua** (OFL) only for the logo and the big home headline; keep **Inter** for body and tables (do not use a decorative face in tables); use a runic Unicode accent set (Noto Sans Runic) for tiny ornament such as zone banners.

Panel and border treatment: 1 px iron border plus a 2 px `--zone-accent` top edge; header with a subtle wood-grain texture as a tiny (<2 KB) tiled SVG or CSS gradient (no photo); parchment panels (dark ink text) for lore or notes, used sparingly; corner "rivets" via `::before` on cards.

Hover and focus: lift 1-2 px with a zone-coloured glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--zone-accent), 0 4px 16px var(--zone-glow)`); keep the existing amber focus ring; all motion stays under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Small tasteful animation: ember pulse on the active nav item, a 200 ms "stamp" when ticking a checklist box, a progress ring that fills in 400 ms. Nothing that loops on screen while someone is playing.

Badges and tiers: hoard badges as small stamped tags with an icon (anvil "Must keep", chest "Keep some", bin "Safe to discard", trophy "Decorative", coin "Sell") and a distinct shape per type so colour is never the only signal; tier colours for item names by the zone they unlock (Meadows green, Black Forest teal, Swamp olive, Mountains frost, Plains gold), also marked with a number so it passes colour-blind checks.

Tone of voice (keep it short and always clear first, joke second):
- 404: "Lost in the Black Forest. There is nothing at this address. Back to the longhouse (start page)."
- Empty search: "No results. Even Odin's ravens found nothing. Try fewer letters, or check the spelling."
- Empty checklist filter: "Nothing here yet. Go hit something."
- Unknown source: "Source unknown. The skalds have not told us yet."
- Offline toast: "Ready for the long winter (works offline)."
- Tooltips: plain text only; do not put jokes where data is.

## 5. Verdict, first five changes, questions

**Overall verdict.** The foundation is genuinely good: a disciplined data pipeline, honest "unverified" handling, a validate step that catches real errors, 182 passing tests, clean accessibility and no runtime errors. The earlier review's biggest problems were fixed quickly. What stops it from being trustworthy and pleasant right now is a handful of gaps that a player hits first: the Smelter has no recipe, a returning visitor can be running last week's data without being told, search fails on short typos, the item page hides the answer under a progress panel, and the first-visit download is twice as large as it needs to be. Visually it is competent and bland: it reads as an admin dashboard with Valheim icons, and the zone structure of the game is not used at all.

**The five changes I would make first**
1. Add Surtling and Surtling Core, rebuild, and fix the station build costs and the stale "unknown" wording (F1).
2. Stop inlining icons and trim the bundle; switch to auto-update or a persistent update banner, and show the data version on every screen size (F2, F3).
3. Fix search: fuzzy for 3+ letters, OR fallback, aliases, "did you mean" (F5).
4. Re-order the item page and clean the hoard defaults and notes (F6, F7, F8).
5. Resolve the missing sources for Fenris Hair, Fenris Claw and Ymir Flesh by reading the raw drop tables, and model trinkets (F4, F9).

3. Should crafted gear show no hoard badge at all, or a neutral "Gear" badge (F7)? And should Coins become "keep some"?
4. Which direction for the look: full "longhouse" theming (top 10 items 1, 5, 6) or a lighter pass first (items 5, 6, 10 only)? Do you want zone colours on the whole page or only on banners and accents?

9. The site is on the same `github.io` origin as your other projects. Do you want to keep that, or move the valheim site to its own domain or user page (F20)?
